## Release blockers and follow-up

1. Replace TM-044, TM-065 and TM-005 with appropriate brand-neutral or genuinely licensed product-context imagery. The present photographs misleadingly associate identifiable third-party equipment with invented repair, footwear and navigation products.
2. Inspect actual loaded-media layouts: hero focal point behind text at 320px/390px/768px and desktop, and 4:5 product-card crops. Stress-test crops are not proof of the actual CSS.
3. Resolve the exact source photo page, creator, license, depicted-person/property/trademark constraints and commercial redistribution rights for every retained source. General Unsplash licensing does not settle third-party product representations.
4. Produce reviewed optimized local derivatives from approved originals and record hashes and final alt text before retiring remote CDN references.
5. Continue next with muskoka-retreat (five photo identities), then creative-agency's film/poster.
