## Licensing context from original publisher

- [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) broadly permits commercial photo use and modification but restricts selling images without significant modification and creating stock-service equivalents.
- [Releases and Trademarks](https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612329-releases-and-trademarks) expressly warns that identifiable people, private/restricted property and brands can require separate rights and releases even though the photo has a copyright license.
- [Unsplash+ License](https://unsplash.com/plus/license) separately prohibits using Unsplash+ images in digital templates. The two original pages here were labelled free standard Unsplash; **do not assume all other unverified CDN images share that entitlement**.
- Attribution helps source tracking but does not replace the need to evaluate commercial template redistribution, depiction and false endorsement.
