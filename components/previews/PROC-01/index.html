01
FIRST CONVERSATION

Discover

Understanding the space, the priorities, and what matters most.

What happens here?

We discuss the scope, the people using the space, and practical requirements before proposing next steps.

02
PLANNING & DESIGN

Define

Bringing the ideas into a focused, achievable direction.

What happens here?

Project requirements, choices, estimated timing, and scope are clarified. No actual design engagement is represented in this demo.

03
CONSTRUCTION

Build

Carrying out the work with attention to materials and finish.

What happens here?

Work progresses through agreed milestones, with site communication and quality reviews appropriate to the project.

04
HANDOVER

Settle in

Reviewing the final work and preparing the space for everyday life.

What happens here?

The finished spaces are walked through, outstanding items reviewed, and care information provided as needed.
