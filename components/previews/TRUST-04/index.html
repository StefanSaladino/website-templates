Where does real data come from?

Use a traceable source—an internal project log or audited report—and document the exact period and inclusion rules.

How should examples be labeled?

Every figure shown here is simulated. A production page must not imply these are the business's measured results.

What should not be claimed?

A sample percentage must never become a fabricated guarantee, testimonial or externally verified performance claim.
