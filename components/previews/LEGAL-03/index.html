03 / WHAT TO COMMUNICATE

A statement
should be specific.

On a live site, identify the actual operator, the accessibility standard and level being assessed where applicable, the date and method of evaluation, known limitations and a usable method of receiving feedback.

What if there is a known barrier?

Explain the specific issue in understandable language, provide an accessible alternative, and keep the status current. Avoid claiming a successful audit without completing one.

Where should feedback go?

To a named responsible team or accessible form with a documented response process. This component has no real support team or inbox.
