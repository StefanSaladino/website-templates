## Revision included in this handoff

- Hero is locked to one full visible viewport below the sticky header on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Mobile hero is intentionally recomposed as a full-viewport image/copy overlay rather than a collapsed desktop stack.
- Product image rail expanded from 4 to 8 items, with additional cook, camp, navigation and repair imagery.
- Hero headline sizing/column ratios were reworked to prevent the copy from breaking into awkward narrow fragments at laptop and wide-desktop widths.
- Text/grid overflow protections were added across product rows, material rows, repair rows, footer and hero metadata.
- Responsive rhythm was tuned at 320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1366, 1440 and 1920 px widths.
- Gallery thumbnail proportions were adjusted to match the revised desktop hero.
