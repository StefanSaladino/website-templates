05 / EXAMPLE PROCESS

Four steps.
No mystery.

  1. 01

    Send the idea

    In a real studio, a consultation might begin with a placement, rough size, references and artist preferences. This demo does not deliver enquiries.

  2. 02

    We match the work

    A real studio would confirm an available qualified artist, policies, timing and any deposit before proceeding. BLACKLINE cannot issue appointments or payment requests.

  3. 03

    Draw for the body

    An actual artist would develop artwork for the person and its placement, subject to consultation and consent.

  4. 04

    Tattoo day

    A real appointment would include a consent process, safety checks, final positioning and professional aftercare instructions.
