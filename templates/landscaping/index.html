01 / WHAT WE BUILD

The whole yard,
or just the part
that isn’t working.

Some projects begin with an empty backyard. Others begin with one soggy corner, a patio that feels stranded, or a front walk that never quite made sense.

A

Planting + garden beds

Trees, shrubs, grasses and perennials chosen for the site — not just the nursery bench.

B

Patios + walkways

Natural stone and pavers laid out to feel connected to the house and the rest of the garden.

C

Grading + drainage

Water gets somewhere sensible to go before the pretty part starts.

D

Garden resets

Thin, divide, move, remove and replant when a mature yard needs editing rather than replacing.

FIELD NOTE

Good landscaping usually means doing less, more deliberately. Keep what works. Fix what doesn’t. Let plants have enough room to become plants.

03 / PLANT FOR THE YEAR

Not just for
the week it’s installed.

Evergreens, bark, seed heads, branch structure and fall colour matter too. A planting plan should still have something to say after the flowers are finished.
