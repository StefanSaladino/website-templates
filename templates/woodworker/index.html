02 / THE SHOP

Measure twice.
Then make it fit.

The point of a small shop is flexibility. A shelf can be four inches shorter. A planter can clear the railing. A bench can stop exactly before the heat register.

“Custom” usually starts with a tape measure, not a mood board.
