02 / The twilight zone

Light
falls away.

As sunlight weakens, colour disappears. Below roughly 200 metres, there is too little light for most photosynthesis. The ocean becomes a place of silhouettes.

03 / The midnight zone

Life makes
its own light.

Sunlight no longer reaches this part of the ocean. Many deep-sea animals use bioluminescence to communicate, find prey or evade predators.

A world
