From the kitchen

Made by hand,
not pulled from
a freezer.

This is the kind of baking where your order has its own mixing bowl, its own piping bag and usually a scrap of paper nearby with your colour notes written on it.

That means a smaller number of orders each week — and a little more care put into each one.

♥

“Tell me who it’s for and what they love. We’ll make the rest feel like them.”

How ordering works

Easy as pie.
Almost.

  1. ✿
    Step one

    Send the date

    Tell me the event date, what you’re thinking of ordering and roughly how many people you’re feeding.

  2. ✿
    Step two

    Make it yours

    Share colours, references, a party invitation or even just a few words. You don’t need a finished design.

  3. ✿
    Step three

    Save your spot

    Once the details and pickup window are settled, a deposit holds your date and it goes onto the bake calendar.
