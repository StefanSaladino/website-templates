NIGHT 02 · NORTH SHORE

Cold starts.
Warm hands.

There is a point late in the day when every extra thing in the pack starts arguing for itself. We spent three wet nights north of Superior cutting the kit down to what still felt useful after dark.

“Good gear disappears into the trip. Bad gear becomes the trip.”
What made the cut ↘

MATERIAL INDEX

Chosen for what happens after year one.

Every material has a repair path. If a zipper, sole or panel can be replaced, the rest of the product gets to keep going.
