# Campaign artwork
Espresso and Filter packaging scenes are generated photographic-style assets for the fictional STICAZZI COFFEE brand. Portrait images are crops, and the Espresso clean/bag layers are extracted from the original generated plate. No 3D assets.

S05a Ritual: the cup and saucer are extracted from the user-supplied `IMG_4331.jpeg` (399 × 501, source photographer and publication rights not independently verified). Original ceramic and coffee pixels are retained with a manually traced alpha matte, warm colour correction, resampling and composited contact shadow. The stone background is a separately generated campaign asset. Commercial/public gallery release is subject to confirming reuse rights.

S08c: existing real cup and background assets retained; fixed composition, tonal adjustment and reduced displayed size in CSS. No new photo or model; prior rights limitation unchanged.

S08c2: user-approved AI-generated photographic recreation of the original cup and saucer reference, 1448 × 1086, with a complete warm stone environment and integrated shadow. Not an exact upscaled copy or original photograph. Target asset ritual-espresso.webp. Prior source photograph remains in repository history.

S08d: closing artwork reuses the intact original generated Espresso hero.webp, with a right-aligned crop only; no new source artwork, composites or additional rights claims.

S08d2: closing now reprises approved ritual-espresso.webp, full frame; retired the levitating-bag hero photo from this chapter only. No new image assets.

S08d3: new AI-generated photographic closing still life, distinct from hero and Ritual assets; kraft STICAZZI COFFEE Espresso packaging, seated flat on stone with cup and beans. Artwork is fictional and its label is a close recreation rather than exact product photography. WebP optimized from generated 1448×1086 PNG.
