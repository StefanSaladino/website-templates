Concept case study / Botanical skincare

VESSA.

A simple silhouette remains legible at small scale, while the wordmark can carry the brand when the symbol is not needed.

Scope Visual identityRole Design & art directionStatus Fictional brief
VESSA
01 / The brief

The challenge.

Create a quietly distinctive skincare identity that feels considered rather than clinical. It must work on a tiny label, on a shelf and in digital applications.

02 / The response

The creative direction.

A tapered V becomes a botanical silhouette. A restrained, high-contrast wordmark balances the organic symbol with a disciplined editorial grid.

Symbol / 01
#422E26
#E8DECF
#AD7965
03 / The delivery

A coherent system.

Primary and compact marks, a warm neutral palette, label hierarchy, single-colour artwork and a compact visual usage guide.

04 / The rationale

Why it works.

A simple silhouette remains legible at small scale, while the wordmark can carry the brand when the symbol is not needed.

Concept project. No real client or measured business outcome is represented.
