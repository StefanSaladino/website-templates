VESSA

Botanical skincare · Identity concept

Explore the brief ↓
Stock photograph / Material reference

01 / The design process

From brief to identity.

Fictional design brief. Original vector concept artwork is shown below; the preceding photograph illustrates the material context, not a finished client deliverable.

01 / The brief
The challenge.

Create a quietly distinctive skincare identity that feels considered rather than clinical. It must work on a tiny label, on a shelf and in digital applications.

02 / The response
The creative direction.

A tapered V becomes a botanical silhouette. A restrained, high-contrast wordmark balances the organic symbol with a disciplined editorial grid.

Symbol / 01
#422E26
#E8DECF
#AD7965
03 / The delivery
A coherent system.

Primary and compact marks, a warm neutral palette, label hierarchy, single-colour artwork and a compact visual usage guide.

04 / The rationale
Why it works.

A simple silhouette remains legible at small scale, while the wordmark can carry the brand when the symbol is not needed.

Concept project. No real client or measured business outcome is represented.

CANTO

Coffee & bakery · Identity concept

Explore the brief ↓
Stock photograph / Material reference

02 / The design process

From brief to identity.

Fictional design brief. Original vector concept artwork is shown below; the preceding photograph illustrates the material context, not a finished client deliverable.

01 / The brief
The challenge.

Develop a coffee and bakery identity with enough personality to stand out on takeaway cups and storefront signage, without leaning on familiar coffee-bean imagery.

02 / The response
The creative direction.

A bold C forms an open circular gesture. A horizontal detail evokes a table or counter. The typography and terracotta palette reinforce warmth and everyday familiarity.

Symbol / 01
#843B32
#F7E2BB
#BC795B
03 / The delivery
A coherent system.

Wordmark, standalone emblem, cup sleeve layout, paper bag pattern, menu typography and two-colour print guidelines.

04 / The rationale
Why it works.

The strong shapes translate easily to one-colour printing, while the repeated emblem creates a recognizable packaging rhythm.

Concept project. No real client or measured business outcome is represented.

Arts festival concept · Identity concept

Explore the brief ↓
Stock photograph / Material reference

03 / The design process

From brief to identity.

Fictional design brief. Original vector concept artwork is shown below; the preceding photograph illustrates the material context, not a finished client deliverable.

01 / The brief
The challenge.

Design a flexible identity for a fictional multidisciplinary arts festival, recognizable across event posters, schedules and digital listings while accommodating varied artists.

02 / The response
The creative direction.

An arch becomes a modular graphic unit. Repetition and changing scale create a visual language that can be recomposed without losing its identity.

Symbol / 01
#2C2843
#D2CFED
#E2AA6C
03 / The delivery
A coherent system.

Festival symbol, typographic system, poster family, schedule layout and social-format artwork.

04 / The rationale
Why it works.

The identity depends on a consistent visual rule instead of one fixed poster, allowing different events to share a common voice.

Concept project. No real client or measured business outcome is represented.

About the designerIndependent practice

Less noise.
More meaning.

I approach design as a process of reduction: finding the essential idea, then expressing it with clarity, craft and personality.

My focus is visual identity, typography and print-led art direction. This fictional portfolio demonstrates how a designer can organize and present their own work.

Available for identity and print-led projects. Contact details are intentionally omitted from this fictional template.
