02 / ONE MATERIAL, MANY STATES

The crease is
the composition.

Design doesn't have to end at the edge of the page. The printed composition changes as its surface moves: a letter disappears into a valley, reappears on a ridge, and becomes readable as the paper opens.

Every panel in the interactive study is attached to its neighbour. Changing the fold angle changes the geometry, not the image.
