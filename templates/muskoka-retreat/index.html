01 / THE HOUSE

Not much between
you and the lake.

Morrow House sits close to the shoreline, tucked into pine and granite. The rooms are simple, warm and deliberately quiet. Most of the attention is pointed outside.

THE ORIGINAL HOUSESix rooms.
