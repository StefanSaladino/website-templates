01 / THE COURTYARD HOUSEExterior
Original architectural illustrationCONCEPT / NOT TO SCALE
Architecture viewing options

Two wings enclose a terrace and reflecting pool. A continuous roof establishes a horizontal line against the landscape.

02 / Spatial principles

The courtyard
is the room.

Rather than filling the plot, the house makes space for an absence. Living spaces turn towards water, stone and a sheltered garden, with the roof extending into the landscape.

Light becomes a material. The depth of the overhang changes how the spaces are experienced throughout the day.
