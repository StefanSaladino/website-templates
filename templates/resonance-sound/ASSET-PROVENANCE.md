# RESONANCE
Original code-authored particle installation and synthesized sine tone. Uses idealized Chladni-style cosine-mode difference as a nodal attractor, finite-difference gradients and bounded stochastic agitation. Not a physical ferrofluid simulation or calibrated experimental plate. Frequency selects discrete modes, intensity controls visual force and conservative output gain, live analyser energy modulates particle luminance/agitation. No external media, microphone, uploads or autoplay audio. All browser-local. Fallback and thumbnail are original schematic SVG artwork.
