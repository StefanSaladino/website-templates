HOW A PROJECT MOVES

Four handoffs. One accountable team.

  1. 01

    Walk the space

    We meet at the house, talk through priorities and identify the parts of the project that need design, engineering or permits.

    Site visit
  2. 02

    Build the scope

    Selections, allowances and trade work are written down before the construction agreement is signed.

    Planning
  3. 03

    Run the build

    Trades move from one shared schedule. Changes are discussed before they become invoices.

    Construction
  4. 04

    Close every detail

    Final walkthrough, deficiency list and handoff happen before the project is considered complete.

    Handover
