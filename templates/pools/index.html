05 / FROM DIRT TO WATER

Four turns
of the tide.

  1. 01

    Site + concept

    Measure the yard, understand access, setbacks and grades, then settle the pool’s basic position and proportion.

  2. 02

    Design + selections

    Depths, steps, coping, interior finish, equipment and surrounding materials are coordinated before excavation.

  3. 03

    Excavate + build

    Dig, structure, plumbing, electrical, concrete and deck work happen in the right order.

  4. 04

    Fill + handoff

    Water goes in, equipment is commissioned and you learn the system before the first real swim.
